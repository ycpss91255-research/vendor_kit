ci(release-test-tools): build arm64 on a native runner instead of QEMU
## Problem

`release-test-tools.yaml` still uses `docker/setup-qemu-action` for its
arm64 leg (`platforms: linux/amd64,linux/arm64` on a single runner with
QEMU emulation). But `build-worker.yaml` and `publish-worker.yaml` have
already moved to a **native-runner matrix** (`linux/arm64 ->
ubuntu-24.04-arm`, no emulation). `release-test-tools` is the only
workflow still on QEMU -- slower and inconsistent with the rest.

## Required

Convert the `release-test-tools` arm64 leg to a native `ubuntu-24.04-arm`
runner matrix mirroring build-worker / publish-worker, and drop
`docker/setup-qemu-action`.

## Umbrella (follow-up)

This is the seed of a "CI native-runner convergence" umbrella. A later
item under the same umbrella: share a single source for the
platform->runner mapping (today build-worker / publish-worker each inline
their own parser, and #579 A2 adds another static matrix) instead of
duplicating it per workflow.
