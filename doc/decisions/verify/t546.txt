refactor(make): retire Makefile wrapper + downstream fanout (phase 2 of #475, ADR-00000005)
## Background

ADR-00000005 (#475) decided to adopt `just` over the GNU make wrapper, in a two-phase rollout. This issue is **phase 2: retire the Makefile wrapper and roll the removal to all 13 downstream repos**.

**Blocked by phase 1** (introduce the `justfile` additively in `base`). The retirement is **bound to the downstream fanout**: the Makefile is symlinked (`Makefile -> .base/script/docker/Makefile`), not copied, so removing it from `base` breaks every downstream simultaneously unless the fanout lands in lockstep.

## Scope

- Remove `script/docker/Makefile` from `base` and drop its `init.sh` symlink creation.
- Retire the #414 / #448 / #469 workarounds **with** the wrapper (the clean `just` recipe + trailing-args passthrough replaces the `MAKEOVERRIDES` guard, the mandatory `--` separator, and the `EXEC_ARGS` shim) -- removed with the wrapper, not reverted in isolation (per #475).
- Grep-and-update sweep across `.github/workflows/` (and helper scripts) + IDE task configs (`.vscode/tasks.json` etc.) re-pointing `make <verb>` callsites at `just <verb>`.
- Doc churn: README + `README.zh-TW.md` / zh-CN / ja + CHANGELOG (`make X` -> `just X`), in `base` and downstream.
- 13-repo batch base-tag fanout (`/batch-base-upgrade` + `/batch-pr`) carrying the removal.

## Out of scope

- `Makefile.ci` stays on make (out of scope per #475 / ADR-00000005).

## TDD

Strict red->green for the `base`-side changes (init.sh symlink test updates, etc.); the fanout is mechanical per-repo.
